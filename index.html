WELCOME TO MY FIRST JAVASCRIPT PROJECT

ROCK-PAPER-SCISSOR CONSOLE BASED

HOW TO PLAY:

  1. Open the browser console (Press F12 and select the Console tab).
  2. Refresh the page to start a new game.
  3. When prompted, enter one of the following:
    • rock
    • paper
    • scissor
  4. The computer will randomly choose its move.
  5. The winner for that round will be displayed with the user and computer's choice.
  6. Scores are displayed after each round.
  7. At the end of the 5 rounds, the overall winner is announced.

Rules:

  • Rock beats Scissor
  • Scissor beats Paper
  • Paper beats Rock

Good luck and have fun 🎉
